About This Assessment

The Spiritual Gifts Questionnaire is grounded in biblical theology and recognizes 23 distinct spiritual gifts. These gifts represent various ways God equips believers to serve and build up the church.

Key Features:

  • 92 Reflective Questions: Each question is carefully worded to help you reflect on your actual experiences and inclinations.
  • 23 Spiritual Gifts: Comprehensive identification of gifts including helps, leadership, hospitality, serving, administration, discernment, faith, music, miracles, craftsmanship, healing, giving, mercy, wisdom, knowledge, exhortation, teaching, shepherding, apostleship, missions, prophecy, evangelism, and prayer.
  • Automatic Progress Saving: Your answers are saved as you progress, allowing you to return and complete the assessment later if needed.
  • Detailed Report: Upon completion, you'll receive a comprehensive report showing your top three gifts along with biblical descriptions and references.
  • Exportable Results: Print, save as PDF, or email your results for future reference.

How to Use This Questionnaire

Step 1: Select your preferred language and click "Begin Assessment."

Step 2: Enter your name (optional) to personalize your report.

Step 3: For each statement, rate your level of agreement on a scale between Strongly Agree and Strongly Disagree. Answer based on your honest reflection of your actual experiences and inclinations—not how you think you should be.

Step 4: Your progress is automatically saved. You can take breaks and return to complete the assessment whenever you're ready.

Step 5: Upon completion, review your personalized report. Your top three gifts are highlighted with full biblical descriptions.

Step 6: Print, save as PDF, or email your results for your records and to share with your faith community leaders.
